How to use TrueScale

True scale: calibrate once (Calibrate → match the bar to a real ruler or bank card). After that, 1″ on the drawing = 1″ on the glass in 1:1 mode — hold a real scale right against the screen.

Measuring tools (readouts in inches, thou, and mm):
Press the drawing and a magnifier bubble appears — drag to aim exactly, lift your finger to place the point. Two fingers pans the page while a tool is active.
↔ Distance — two points.
∠ Angle — one end, then the corner, then the other end.
◯ Circle — three points anywhere on a circle or arc; you get radius and diameter.
Undo removes the last point or measurement; Clear wipes them all.

📏 Ruler: one finger moves it; two fingers twist it like a real ruler (snaps at 45°). The round ⟳ handle also spins it — a quick tap on it steps 45°. The 16ths / .001″ button switches its inch edge between fractions and decimal — in 2:1 view the decimal edge shows true 10-thou ticks.

Drawing scale: if the sheet is marked e.g. SCALE 1:2, set it here and every readout converts to real part size. Angles never change.

Offline: every PDF you open is stored on this device and reopens by itself at your last page — no internet needed. In Chrome, use menu → Add to Home screen to install it like an app.
